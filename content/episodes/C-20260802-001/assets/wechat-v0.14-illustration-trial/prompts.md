# 配图提示词记录｜V0.14

## 候选一｜付款前的手指悬停

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide 3:2 editorial lifestyle image for a WeChat article, displayed around 375 px wide
Primary request: Create a believable late-night purchase-decision scene with restrained visual satire. On a warm desk, an adult hand pauses just before touching a generic smartphone payment button. Nearby, an absurdly oversized gift bow, an almost-empty hourglass, repeated sale reminder tabs, and an over-polished wellness brochure make an ordinary checkout feel like a theatrical “last chance” ritual. The viewer should feel the pressure, but the consumer must remain dignified.
Scene/backdrop: lived-in home desk at night, warm paper notes, ceramic cup and quiet domestic details; no identifiable room, institution, person or product
Style/medium: natural editorial lifestyle photography with one slightly absurd visual metaphor, realistic textures, not glossy commercial advertising
Composition/framing: wide 3:2; phone and hovering hand around the center-left; keep a calm, readable lower-right surface with at least 8% safe margin for a later wordmark; full hand and phone visible; no crop
Lighting/mood: warm practical lamp light, alert but humane, dryly humorous rather than frightening
Color palette: warm paper #F5F1E8, deep ink green #152B24, brand green #1E493C, muted sage #ADC3B5, restrained warm orange #E86F3E
Text: none
Constraints: generic blurred interface shapes only; no readable UI copy; no medical claims; no implication of efficacy; no logo, watermark or brand mark
Avoid: hospital blue, white coat, pill, supplement bottle, DNA, organs, black-gold anti-aging advertising, fear-based aging imagery, luxury clinic, identifiable person, mocking expression, extra fingers
```

## 候选二｜从论文到眼前项目的四道核对

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: text-free wide 3:2 editorial visual body for a hybrid WeChat explainer
Primary request: Build a physical editorial tabletop model of an evidence-checking path. Arrange four clearly separated stations from left to right: a stack of research papers under a magnifying lens; a small generic product or device model being compared; a hands-on usage path with instruction pieces; and a final everyday-life outcome marker that still needs verification. Connect the stations with a restrained path interrupted by visible inspection gates, so the image clearly says “each step must be checked” and never suggests that a paper directly proves a consumer health result.
Scene/backdrop: warm off-white paper studio and editorial worktable, tactile paper, wood, matte ceramic and soft gray materials
Style/medium: refined editorial physical-model illustration, lightly dimensional but not generic glossy 3D, with hand-made research-desk character
Composition/framing: wide 3:2; all four stations fully visible across the upper two-thirds; generous left and right margins; clean lower area; no crop; each station visually distinct
Lighting/mood: soft studio light, calm, rigorous, slightly skeptical
Color palette: warm paper #F5F1E8, ink green #152B24, brand green #1E493C, muted sage #ADC3B5, warm orange #E86F3E used only for inspection gates
Text: absolutely no letters, words, numbers, paper titles, UI labels or logos
Constraints: no direct arrow from paper to success; no implied medical efficacy; no identifiable product, institution, researcher or patient; four stations only
Avoid: generated Chinese, English, DNA, organs, white coat, hospital blue, black-gold anti-aging, generic AI dashboard, pills, supplement packaging, dramatic miracle glow, watermark
```
